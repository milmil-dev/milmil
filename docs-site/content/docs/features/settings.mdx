## Overview

The Settings page is organized into tabbed panels, each covering a distinct area of configuration. Open it from the sidebar or navigation menu.

## Panels

### General

Set your preferred **display language** (EN, JA, ZH-CN, ZH-TW, ZH-HK, KO), choose the **week start day** for calendar views, and toggle **auto-add to collection** so that newly matched anime are added to your collection automatically.

### Player

Customize subtitle appearance — **font size**, **font family**, and **style presets** — and review the default **keyboard shortcuts** for playback control (play/pause, seek, volume, fullscreen, etc.).

### Account

Manage your **username**, generate or revoke **API tokens** for third-party access, and view or terminate active **sessions**.

### Integrations

Connect external services:

| Service | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **DandanPlay** | File-hash matching and danmaku (bullet comments) |
| **Trakt.tv** | Watch history sync via OAuth device-code flow |

<Callout type="info">
Trakt.tv uses the device-code flow — milmil displays a code you enter at trakt.tv/activate on any browser.
</Callout>

### Downloads

View the current **download engine status** and run a **health check** to verify that the torrent client and feed subscriptions are operating normally.

### Notifications

Configure **push** and **email** notification preferences on a per-event basis (e.g., new episodes available, download complete, scan finished).

### Backup

Schedule automated backups to **WebDAV** or **S3-compatible** storage. Set the backup frequency and retention policy to protect your database and configuration.

<Callout type="warn">
Backups include your database and settings but not media files. Ensure your media storage has its own redundancy.
</Callout>

### Storage

Review **cache statistics** and **storage usage** across all configured backends. Use the **clear cache** action to reclaim disk space used by thumbnails and temporary files.

### About

Displays the current **app version**, open-source **license**, and project **credits**.
